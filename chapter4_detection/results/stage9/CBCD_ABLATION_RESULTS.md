# CBCD Ablation Results

The preregistered gate required stable positive CBCD evidence in both overall performance and localization quality. It was not met:

- seed42 mAP50-95 was lower than its matched E0;
- AP75 decreased on two of three seeds and in the three-seed mean;
- source-group bootstrap intervals crossed zero;
- low-contrast Black improvement was not repeatable.

Therefore A1 was intentionally not run. Searching variants after this mixed result would violate the validation-search constraint.
