# Gate contribution

For identical features and projection, suppression `S=1-||r phi(D)||/(||phi(D)||+eps)` was evaluated directly. Initialization-scale effects are mostly 1–2%. Training-derived features expand the effect, but at 1536 the mean is 4.06% Flash, 5.68% Black, 5.40% low-contrast Black, 3.02% G2 and 4.89% G3.

Therefore r is not merely a universal .99 multiplier after training, but its additional action beyond D is not usefully selective: real Black—including the low-contrast subgroup—is suppressed at least as much as the high-response background group. Feature-norm suppression is not interpreted as a class-score or AP improvement.
