# QPR-CIR literature positioning

A sigmoid gate, learned box refinement and localization-quality estimation are established ideas; QPR-CIR cannot claim novelty from gating alone. [IoU-Net (ECCV 2018)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_ECCV_2018/html/Borui_Jiang_Acquisition_of_Localization_ECCV_2018_paper.html) predicts localization IoU for ranking/NMS and optimization-based refinement. [BBRefinement (2022)](https://doi.org/10.3390/app12073402) separately refines detector boxes from object crops. [CLQ (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neucom.2024.127504) estimates localization quality to improve detection confidence. QPR-CIR instead freezes scores, classes, candidates and CIR, and predicts only how much of an existing residual to apply; its narrower hypothesis is that paired original/reconstructed CIR features reveal whether that residual is safe. GateFull does not outperform GateGeo or uniform attenuation here, so the cross-resolution quality-control distinction is not empirically established and novelty remains unconfirmed.
